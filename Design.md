# Finance & Business Tracking Website Blueprint

## 1. Home Page (Main Overview)

This is the first thing users see. It should give a full picture of their money in one place.

### What it shows:

* Total money available (everything combined)
* Money coming in vs money going out
* Current savings or balance
* Quick summary of all activity
* Progress toward financial goals
* A simple breakdown of:

  * Personal money
  * Each business (if any)

### Purpose:

To answer one question instantly:

> “How am I doing financially right now?”

---

## 2. Personal Finance Section

This is where users manage their everyday money.

### What they can do:

* Add money they receive (salary, gifts, etc.)
* Record money they spend (food, transport, rent, etc.)
* See where their money is going
* Set a monthly spending limit per category
* View simple charts of spending habits

### Purpose:

Help users understand and control personal spending.

---

## 3. Goals Section

This is for saving and planning.

### What users can do:

* Set goals (example: buy phone, save tuition, emergency fund)
* Set how much they need and by when
* Track progress automatically as they save
* See how close they are to reaching each goal

### Purpose:

Help users stay motivated and disciplined with money.

---

## 4. Business Section (Separate Area)

This is for users who run one or more businesses.

Each business is treated like its own space.

### Inside each business:

* Money coming in (sales, income)
* Money going out (expenses, costs)
* Profit tracking
* List of transactions
* Optional: inventory or stock tracking
* Optional: invoices or customer payments

### Key idea:

Each business is separate from personal money and from other businesses.

---

## 5. Business List Page

If a user has more than one business:

### They see:

* A list of all their businesses
* Each business as its own card
* Clicking one opens its full details

### Purpose:

Keep everything organized and separate.

---

## 6. Combined Financial View

A special summary view that combines everything.

### Shows:

* Total personal + business money
* Total income from all sources
* Total spending across everything
* Overall profit or loss

### Purpose:

Give a “big picture” of all finances in one place.

---

## 7. Settings & Control Area

### Users can:

* Add or remove businesses
* Set currency or preferences
* Control notifications (like overspending alerts)
* Manage account safety

---

# Core Idea Behind the Whole System

Think of it like this:

* Personal money = everyday life
* Businesses = separate money worlds
* Goals = future plans
* Home page = overall scoreboard

Everything is connected, but clearly separated so nothing gets confusing.

---

# What makes this strong

* Simple for beginners
* Powerful for business owners
* Clear separation of money types
* One place to see everything
* Encourages better money habits
